Research

Research

Making virtual humans aware of their surroundings, putting them in front of real caregivers, and measuring what actually changes.

  • Dissertation · In progress

    Aware Intelligent Virtual Humans in Mixed Reality

    Automating spatial awareness in embodied virtual humans so that context-appropriate non-verbal behavior is generated without a human operator in the loop.

    The automated system is benchmarked against a human-controlled baseline on non-verbal response accuracy and system latency, then evaluated in a user study measuring perceived accuracy, latency, and realism.

  • NSF FW-HTF · 2022–present

    Embodied Virtual Human Simulation for Caregiver Training

    Student team lead on the NSF Future of Work at the Human-Technology Frontier collaborative project (Awards #2222661, #2222662, #2222663) spanning NJIT, the University of Central Florida, and the University of Delaware.

    Prototyped an interactive AR/XR/MR simulation featuring a spatially aware embodied geriatric virtual patient for caregiver communication and soft-skills training, built in Unity 3D and C# for the Microsoft HoloLens 2.

  • Methods

    Mixed-Methods Evaluation of Immersive Systems

    Quantitative user studies of system usability, technology acceptance, social presence, and caregiver soft skills including empathy, attitudes, and self-efficacy.

    Qualitative interviews with professional caregivers, analyzed through thematic analysis, feeding findings back into the next round of simulation design.

  • Earlier work

    Dimensionality Reduction for Hyperspectral Imaging

    Hybrid feature selection and feature extraction pipelines (PCA, LDA, mRMR, nMI) paired with SVM, KNN, and ANN classifiers for multi-band satellite imagery.

    Undergraduate thesis research at RUET, which produced three peer-reviewed IEEE and ACM conference publications.
