Asif Ahmmed Joy

Ph.D. Candidate, Information Systems

Department of Informatics · New Jersey Institute of Technology · Newark, NJ, USA

I build and study augmented, extended, and mixed reality simulations with embodied virtual humans, and I use them to train the people who care for older adults.

My work sits at the meeting point of human–computer interaction, healthcare simulation, and applied AI. I prototype spatially aware virtual patients on the Microsoft HoloLens 2 under a National Science Foundation award, then evaluate them with mixed methods — usability, technology acceptance, and social presence studies alongside interviews with professional caregivers.

Actively seeking new opportunities · graduating December 2026

My advisor is Dr. Salam Daher. I expect to graduate in December 2026.

Research

More detail →

Three threads run through my work: making virtual humans aware of their surroundings, putting them in front of real caregivers, and measuring what actually changes.

  • NSF FW-HTF · 2022–present

    Embodied Virtual Human Simulation for Caregiver Training

    Student team lead on the NSF Future of Work at the Human-Technology Frontier collaborative project (Awards #2222661, #2222662, #2222663) spanning NJIT, the University of Central Florida, and the University of Delaware.

    Prototyped an interactive AR/XR/MR simulation featuring a spatially aware embodied geriatric virtual patient for caregiver communication and soft-skills training, built in Unity 3D and C# for the Microsoft HoloLens 2.

    Explore this project →
  • Methods

    Mixed-Methods Evaluation of Immersive Systems

    Quantitative user studies of system usability, technology acceptance, social presence, and caregiver soft skills including empathy, attitudes, and self-efficacy.

    Qualitative interviews with professional caregivers, analyzed through thematic analysis, feeding findings back into the next round of simulation design.
